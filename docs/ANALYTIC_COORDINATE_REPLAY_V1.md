# Independent coordinate meridian replay V1

This is an additive computational scope of ANALYTIC_TRACE_AUDIT_V1. Its numerical
verifier policy is COORDINATE_MERIDIAN_REPLAY_V1, replaying the recorded producer
policy EXPLICIT_COORDINATE_MERIDIAN_V1. It does not modify generation, canonical
CrochetIR, DesignSpec schemas, forward simulation or human-approved goldens.
Audit PASS is neither physical acceptance nor complete V5/V6-V10 acceptance.

## Scope and input ownership

Require the authoritative DesignSpec 1.2 radius/axial coordinate profile and the
existing bounded ideal-target admission. Support simple closed pole-ended
AMIGURUMI_3D meridians with signed cardinal frames. Legacy radius/arclength inputs
remain ambiguous and unsupported by this audit. Other frames and open boundaries
remain explicitly incomplete. Do not synthesize absent proposal artifacts, infer
missing coordinates or migrate stored projects.

Bind all canonical design/material/target/run/source digests and retained raw
proposals through the existing trace audit. Coordinate-only proposal parameters
must record the exact producer version, admitted target hash and integer bracket
ceiling of two steps per side. Partial or false known metadata cannot establish
proof. Unknown parameter semantics remain explicitly incomplete even after every
producer digest is refreshed.

## Numerical reconstruction and independence

The verifier imports neither the producer sampler nor analytic geometry, solver,
count search, placement optimizer, compiler, balanced-course helper or trace
builder. Shared input admission, SemanticValidator, canonical hashing, binary64
conversion and standard-library hypot are explicit common dependencies. The
count/phase replay kernels remain independently implemented and unchanged.

From admitted represented coordinates, compute exact rational differences and
squared distances. Reconstruct the mandated binary64 hypot estimate from those
differences. Independently enumerate binary64 neighbors through their bit
representation, with at most two widening steps per side. Compare their squares
exactly to the authoritative squared distance. Nonfinite brackets cannot certify
an enclosure. This establishes the declared arithmetic policy, not an assumption
about hypot accuracy or a physical distance bound.

Sum approximate lengths and segment enclosure errors rationally, preserving short
segments. Check the configured total-length tolerance and every final conversion
allowance exactly. Round the reported error bound upward when needed. For each
normalized course midpoint, scan the segments independently and interpolate
radius and signed axial position rationally. At an exact knot either adjacent
segment gives that same authoritative point. Compare the binary64 s/radius/axial
outputs and subsequent circumferences as exact rational strings in the trace.
Do not substitute axial displacement for meridional arclength.

All tolerances are the input policy's millimetres, with their original producer
rationale and ownership; this verifier introduces no acceptance epsilon. The
producer's 2E displacement analysis describes its admitted polyline, not V7
coverage, calibrated yarn behavior or physical stitchability.

## Search and proof resources

Replay the existing staged count/phase algorithm, exact windows, objectives,
deterministic ties, layer work, hypothesis prefix, interruptions, global budgets
and ordered proposal membership. Recompute coordinate proposal segment/error
diagnostics and source/run/target bindings. The prototype final-relation auditor
continues to check the same raw construction and preserved provenance after
search PASS; it does not trust a producer link as its relation proof.

The coordinate verifier charges one proof unit per preprocessed segment and one
per segment inspected while locating a sample. These are software work units,
not time or physical units. At most 128 segments and 8,192 reserved course slots
give at most 1,048,576 sample-scan units plus 128 preprocessing units. Count edges,
phase transitions and angle pairs share the existing 6,000,000-unit trace limit.
Standalone claims numerical preparation is separately bounded to 1,000 units
per raw artifact and the same 128-segment ceiling. Target-admission pair work
retains its separate 8,128-pair bound. JSON, proposal and construction ceilings
remain unchanged; no client proof-budget override is accepted.

Exhausted independent proof work is INDETERMINATE, never FAIL or a partial PASS.
Known contradictory supported claims FAIL. Valid unsupported scopes remain
INDETERMINATE. Structured numerical-failure traces require explicit reached-stage
reconstruction or a named incomplete scope; they cannot be called false merely
because the producer's correctly recorded computation failed.

## Evidence and acceptance limits

Coordinate reports add coordinate_sampling evidence: producer/verifier policy
IDs, admitted target hash, segment count, exact and binary64 total length,
conservative segment-error sum, configured tolerance/conversion allowance and
bracket ceiling. Ordinary native sphere reports retain their previous bytes.
The source record is checked for consistency, not authenticated execution.

API, CLI and durable jobs use the existing inspect_analytic_search_trace route.
V5 admits this scoped proof and the existing prototype relation; it still requires
physical feasible-candidate selection and physical verification. Overall remains
NOT_VERIFIED and physical status UNTESTED. V7 sampling/comparison and calibration
are separate work. Exact hand formulas, high-precision independent oracles,
property tests, refreshed malicious digests and installed/live delivery checks
are required validation. Implementation status is recorded in BACKEND_HANDOFF.md.
