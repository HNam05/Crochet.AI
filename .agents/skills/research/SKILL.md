# Crochet research

Read [`docs/RESEARCH.md`](../../../docs/RESEARCH.md) and [`docs/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md`](../../../docs/RESEARCH_QUESTIONS.md).

- Prefer papers, standards, official project pages, repositories, and license texts over summaries.
- Separate established findings, reasonable inference, project decisions, hypotheses, and future work.
- Record enough bibliographic and version information to reproduce the lookup.
- Do not copy external source code. Mathematical ideas may be independently re-derived and specified with attribution.
- Treat missing, restrictive, non-commercial, or ambiguous licenses as a stop condition for code reuse; document uncertainty.
- Do not make an external implementation a mandatory CI oracle.

Return cited findings, applicability limits, license status, and open questions. Do not present a research lead as an adopted design.
